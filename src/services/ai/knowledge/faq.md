# MasterService Platform FAQ

This file is the authoritative knowledge base for the MasterService support
chatbot. Answer ONLY questions about MasterService itself. For topics outside
the platform (politics, medicine, general advice, etc.), politely decline and
ask the user to stay on MasterService-related questions.

## What is MasterService?

MasterService is a marketplace mobile platform that connects Clients who need
a service with Masters (skilled professionals such as plumbers, electricians,
repair techs) and Sellers (product vendors). It supports four roles: Client,
Master, Seller, and Admin.

## Roles

- **Client** — posts service requests (ads) describing a problem, sets a
  budget and location, and hires a Master to solve it.
- **Master** — views available posted jobs (ads), accepts them, completes the
  work and gets paid.
- **Seller** — lists products for sale that Clients can browse and buy.
- **Admin** — manages users, ads, products, categories, transactions, reviews,
  and moderation queue.

## How to post a request (Client)

1. Open the Client tab and tap "Create ad".
2. Write a description (10–1000 characters). Optionally use the "Fill with AI"
   button to auto-suggest category, title, description and price range — you
   can edit the AI suggestions before saving.
3. Set a budget and choose the location.
4. Submit. The ad becomes active and visible to Masters.

## How a Master gets and earns from a job

1. In the Master tab, browse available jobs.  2. Accept a job that fits.
2. When the work is done, mark the job completed from both sides.
3. Funds move from the Client to the Master as a service payment.

Note: Masters pay an acceptance fee when they accept a job. The current fee
is fixed in the system configuration — confirm the exact current value in the
app before quoting it.

## Payments and balance

- All payments are in Uzbek som (UZS).
- Clients top up their balance and pay for services/products from it.
- Transactions are recorded between users (service payments, product sales,
  acceptance fees, and a one-time registration bonus).
- Money can be withdrawn to a bank account/card using the Withdrawal feature
  (Admin reviews withdrawals; processing times may vary based on the bank).

## Registration bonus

New users receive a one-time registration bonus credited to their balance when
a purchase or registration event occurs. This bonus is added only once.

## Products (Seller)

- Sellers create product listings with name, price, stock, images and category.
- Sellers can use AI to auto-generate a description, tags and category from
  the product name and photos, then edit before publishing.
- Clients can browse the shop, view product details, add to favorites and buy
  products.

## Ratings and reviews

- Clients can review a Master/Seller after a completed job or purchase.
- Reviews affect the average rating shown on the profile. Reviews flagged as
  suspicious by AI are kept visible but are excluded from the average rating
  until an Admin reviews them.

## Categories

- Ads and products are organized into categories. Categories support multiple
  languages. When AI suggests a category, it always chooses from the existing
  category list — never outside it.

## Support and languages

- The app supports Uzbek (uz), Russian (ru) and English (en).
- For account issues, contact support through the app.
